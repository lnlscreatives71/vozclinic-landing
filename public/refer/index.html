Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be a VozClinic clinic to refer?
For this reward, yes: the free month is credited to your own subscription, so you need one. If you're not a clinic, the affiliate program pays 20% of the referred clinic's subscription in cash every month instead, and it's open to anyone.
When does the free month get credited?
Once the clinic you referred has subscribed and stayed active for 60 days. That window covers cancellations and refunds. The credit lands on your next invoice.
Is there a limit on how many clinics I can refer?
No. Every clinic that becomes a customer through you adds another free month. Four active referrals is four months of your plan at no cost.
What if the clinic already knew me and contacted you directly?
If they mention you or show the introduction came from you (a screenshot, a forwarded message) and they subscribe, the month counts. If they reach out independently without naming you, it doesn't. Best practice: send me the intro before they contact me on their own.
Can referrals be outside Baja California?
For now we're focused on Baja California. If you know a clinic in CDMX, Guadalajara, Monterrey, or another city, send it anyway and I'll tell you if we open that city. The free month applies to Baja California clinics right now.
Can I earn the free month and the affiliate commission on the same clinic?
No. One clinic earns one reward. Pick whichever suits you: a free month on your own subscription, or cash commission through the affiliate program.
