4. Affiliate links and attribution

We assign you a unique Affiliate Link. Attribution works as follows:

  • The attribution window is 90 calendar days from the first recorded click on your Affiliate Link.
  • Where multiple links are involved, attribution goes to the last Affiliate Link recorded before subscription.
  • Attribution depends on our records. If a visitor blocks cookies, switches device or browser, or arrives through a channel that does not preserve the referrer, the referral may not be recorded.
  • If a clinic arrives another way and names you, tell us in writing before they subscribe. We will review the case in good faith; the decision rests with us.

You may not manipulate attribution. Cookie stuffing, forced redirects, hidden frames, automated clicking, and any technique that records a referral without the visitor's genuine intent are prohibited.

5. What counts as a Qualified Referral

A referral qualifies when the Referred Clinic: (a) arrives within the attribution window; (b) subscribes to a paid VozClinic plan; (c) completes onboarding; and (d) actually pays its first invoice. Trials, demos, quotes, and subscriptions that are never collected do not earn Commission.

6. Exclusions

The following earn no Commission, even where a click is attributed:

  • Clinics that are already VozClinic customers, or were customers within the previous 12 months.
  • Clinics already in an open sales process, or in a documented conversation with us before your referral.
  • Clinics you own, direct, control, or represent, and referrals made to yourself or through third parties for your own benefit.
  • Subscriptions obtained through conduct prohibited under section 9.
  • Subscriptions cancelled, refunded, or charged back before the Commission clears.

7. Commission

For each Qualified Referral, you earn 20% of the Net Amount Collected from that Referred Clinic's monthly subscription.

  • Commission is recurring: it accrues every month the Referred Clinic pays its subscription.
  • Commission continues for as long as the subscription stays active. There is no month cap and no earnings cap.
  • Commission ends with the last collected invoice when the Referred Clinic cancels, suspends, or stops paying. If that clinic returns later, it does not automatically reinstate.
  • Commission is calculated on the monthly subscription. Add-ons, integrations, implementation services, and separately billed items earn no Commission unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • If plan prices change, your 20% applies to the price in effect for each period.

8. Payouts

  • Clearing: each monthly Commission clears 60 calendar days after we collect the matching invoice. That window covers refunds and chargebacks.
  • Cycle: cleared Commissions are paid in the following monthly cycle.
  • Minimum: your balance must reach $100 USD to trigger a payout. Below that, the balance rolls into the next cycle.
  • Payment method: bank transfer or PayPal, as agreed with you. Payment processing fees are yours to bear.
  • Currency: Commissions are calculated and paid in US dollars (USD) unless otherwise agreed.
  • Taxes: you act as an independent contractor. You are responsible for your own taxes, withholdings, and filings in your jurisdiction, and for giving us any tax documentation the law requires. We may hold payouts while that documentation is missing.
  • Adjustments: if a Commission already paid relates to a payment later refunded or charged back, we may offset that amount against future payouts.
