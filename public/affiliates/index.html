Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a clinic to join?
No. The program is open to any person or company that can introduce clinics legitimately. If you're also a clinic customer you can still take part, as long as you don't refer yourself.
How long does the commission last?
For as long as the clinic stays subscribed and paying. If they cancel, your commission ends with their last collected invoice. There's no month cap and no earnings cap.
When do I get paid?
Each commission clears 60 days after we collect the matching invoice. That window covers refunds and chargebacks. Once cleared, it's paid out in the next monthly cycle.
How is my referral tracked?
Through your unique link. Attribution lasts 90 days from the first click and goes to the last affiliate link recorded. If a clinic arrives another way and names you, tell us before they subscribe and we'll review it.
What if the clinic was already talking to you?
It doesn't earn a commission. Clinics already on our waitlist or in an open conversation are excluded from the program. We'll tell you as soon as we spot it.
