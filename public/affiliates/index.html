FAQ

What if the clinic cancels?

Commission is paid on what the clinic actually pays. If it cancels, commissions end with its service. If it cancels or is refunded within the first 60 days, accrued commissions are void.

What if two people refer the same clinic?

The first valid registration wins. That's why you register the clinic before you introduce it.

Do I have to sell or run demos?

No. You make the introduction; VozClinic handles the demo, the proposal, and the setup.

Can I refer clinics outside Baja California?

Yes. The program is open to clinics in Mexico and the United States, subject to the program terms.
